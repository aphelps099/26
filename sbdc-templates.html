Template rules

Don't edit the master. Always duplicate the template before making changes. The master file is the single source of truth — if it gets modified, every future piece starts from a broken foundation.

Lock the grid. Type sizes, margins, and column widths are set to match the brand spec. Adjusting them for a single piece means that piece looks different from every other piece in the network. Consistency is the product.

Colors are not suggestions. The palette is closed: navy, cobalt, berry, evergreen, fog, pool. If a template doesn't include a color, that color doesn't belong in that template.

Photos follow the photo rules. If a template has a photo slot, the image must meet the photography chapter guidelines — real moments, no stock, no staged handshakes. When in doubt, leave the slot empty.

Requesting a new template

What to include. Send the format (print, digital, presentation), the dimensions if you know them, a description of what the piece needs to communicate, and the audience.

Turnaround. Standard template requests take one to two weeks. Rush requests (under five business days) require director approval.

Where to send it. Email marketing@norcalsbdc.org with the subject line "Template Request" and your center name. You'll get a proof for review before the template goes into the shared workspace.

Canva workspace access

Who has access. All NorCal SBDC center directors, marketing leads, and designated advisors. Access is managed through Canva Teams and tied to your norcalsbdc.org email.

How to get access. Email marketing@norcalsbdc.org with your name, center, and role. Access is typically granted within two business days.

What you can do. View all templates, duplicate them to your own drafts, customize the variable fields (name, center, dates, stats), and export to PDF or PNG. You cannot edit, move, or delete master templates.
