Voice Traits

How we sound.

Direct. Lead with the point. Skip the acronym. Cut corporate filler. Say what you mean.

Human. Write like a person, not an institution. Plain language. Real voice. No jargon.

Knowing. Show the work. Don’t show off. Use numbers. Skip the maybes. Be specific.

Optimistic. Be real about the work. Unreasonable about what’s possible. Name the hard part.

Audience

Write to one reader.

Most of the time, that reader is a small business owner — stretched thin, skeptical of jargon, looking for the next move. Sound like a neighbor, not an institution. Lead with what they get, not what we offer. Answer “what’s in it for me?” before they have to ask.

Why · voice rules

Voice has three jobs. Reposition, unify, convert. Shift perception from government program to trusted business growth partner. Give all 16 centers a shared vocabulary. Turn brand awareness into consultation bookings. Every word should advance one of the three.

Lead with the point. Never the acronym. The first sentence does the work — if it needs “Small Business Development Center” to land, the sentence is wrong.

Use Sera italic only for client story headlines and pull quotes. Every other headline is Nova 700. Numbers are always set in Nova at display weight — never in Sera.

Write to one reader. A founder needs different content than a city councilmember. Pick the person on the other side of the screen and stay with them for the whole sentence.

Never publish a generator output without a human pass. Voice, facts, names, and links must be checked. The generator drafts — the editor approves.

Every piece of content ladders to one CTA: talk to an advisor. If the post can’t answer “why book a consultation,” it isn’t finished.

Why · the evidence

The voice on this page isn’t a hunch. It’s the conclusion. Simple, outcome-focused promises outperform clever ones — HBR and McKinsey both. Top-40 brands with consistent messaging deliver almost 2× shareholder return over 20 years.

People is the differentiator. 84% of Americans prefer human advisors over robo-advisors (NerdWallet, 2023). Mentored businesses post a 70% 5-year survival rate vs. 35% without (SCORE, 2022).

Funded is the proof. America’s SBDC clients accessed $7.7B in capital last year. Capitalized firms have 50% higher survival and are 3× more likely to expand.

Connected is the experience. Founders with strong networks grow 3.5× faster (MIT). 46% of entrepreneurs report isolation; community participation lifts mental-health outcomes by 40% (KPMG, 2022).

Consistency is the multiplier. Brands with a unified voice see up to 23% revenue growth (Lucidpress, 2021). The framework is the conclusion, not the assumption.
