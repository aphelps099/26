  WHY · TYPOGRAPHY RULES

Sera italic is an accent, not a voice. Use it on one or two words inside a Nova headline — never the whole line. A full italic headline reads as a quote and loses its instruction.

Display type stacks in two or three short lines, broken at natural breath points. Set flush left. Never center. If a headline needs more words, drop one step in the scale and use H2 or body.

Sera never appears inside H2 section headings. H2 is always clean Nova 600. The reserved registers stay reserved.

Mono is the only family that uppercases by default. Nova headlines stay sentence case. Mono tracks 0.18–0.22em; Nova display tracks negative.
