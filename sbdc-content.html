Three principles.

Every post follows three rules. Authentic in voice. Written for one audience. Private about anything not ours to share.

01
Authentic.

Use your real name. Never post anonymously. Every post reflects who we are as people.

02
Audience.

A founder needs different content than a city councilmember. Write to the person on the other side of the screen.

03
Privacy.

Never disclose personal, confidential, or proprietary information about clients, advisors, partners, or lenders.

  WHY · CONTENT RULES

The generator drafts. The editor approves. Never publish a generator output without a human pass — voice, facts, names, and links must be checked.

Use Sera italic only for client story headlines and pull quotes. Every other headline is Nova 600. Captions, stats, and CTAs stay in Nova.

Numbers belong in Nova at display weight (the $547M tile, the 3,723 jobs tile). Never set numerals in Sera — they read as decoration, not data.

Photos are grayscale with a Navy/Fog veil for legibility. Never publish a client photo without permission. Never crop a face out for layout.

Cross-posting is not posting. Each channel earns its own write-up — LinkedIn long-form, Facebook community, Instagram visual.
