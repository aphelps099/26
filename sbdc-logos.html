Use it right.

Two rules govern every placement: how much room to give the mark, and where to put it on the page.

Clear space

Maintain space equal to the height of the “A” in America’s on all sides. The clear zone scales with the logo — bigger logo, bigger space.

Placement

Set the logo in the upper-left or bottom-left corner of the page, equal distance from both edges. Don’t center it.

Compliance

Acknowledge the funders.

Two requirements cover almost every piece we send out. The rule below tells you when they apply. Everything else is an edge case.

The rule

If a piece goes to a client or partner, include the funder acknowledgment. If it’s a newsletter, workshop promo, or event, add the ADA accommodation.

Internal-only memos are exempt.

Funder acknowledgment
Logos + disclaimer

The SBDC program focuses on advising small business clients located in the NorCal SBDC network coverage area. Funded in part through a grant with the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development. Funded in part through a cooperative agreement with the U.S. Small Business Administration. All opinions, conclusions, or recommendations expressed are those of the author(s) and do not necessarily reflect the views of the SBA, CAOSBA or Cal Poly Humboldt Sponsored Programs Foundation.

ADA accommodation
Icon + standard text

Reasonable accommodations for persons with disabilities will be made if requested at least 72 hours in advance of this event. Send an email with the subject line ACCOMMODATION REQUEST to [center / program contact email and phone].

WHERE TO APPLY

Events & workshops. Add the ADA accommodation to every flyer, registration page, and event-related send. Pair with the SBDC center name and the SBA logo + disclaimer. Use the standard 72-hour-notice ADA copy — replace the bracketed contact placeholder with the host center’s name, email, and phone.

Web & email newsletters. Carry the funder logos (SBA + CAOSBA) and the combined funder disclaimer in the footer of every newsletter and major web page. If a newsletter promotes a workshop or event, add the ADA accommodation directly under the event details.

Regional slide decks. The closing slide carries the full combined funder acknowledgment — paste as-is, do not retypeset. On regionally-funded decks, add the Cal Poly Humboldt logo and disclaimer alongside the funder block on the closing slide.

Internal memos — exempt. Compliance kicks in only when a piece reaches a client or partner. Internal-only memos and team docs do not require either acknowledgment.

SBA logo placement. The SBA mark cannot be placed adjacent to a sponsor or partner logo on the same piece. Keep the SBA logo visually separate — place it in its own zone, not in a row of partner marks.

Four things to never do.

These are the compliance risks people can see immediately. Use the official files instead of fixing a logo by hand.

Do not stretch or condense.

Keep the logo proportions exactly as provided.

Do not place on unapproved colors.

Use approved backgrounds with enough contrast.

Do not add graphics.

No badges, circles, outlines, decorations, or effects.

Do not rotate.

Keep the orientation fixed and horizontal.

  WHY · LOGO RULES

The network logo earns trust by repeating exactly the same way across every channel. A retyped name is not the logo. A screenshot is not the logo. The official file is the logo.

Reverse marks exist so the logo stays legible on Navy, photography, and dark color fields. If contrast is uncertain, switch versions before adjusting the file.

Funder acknowledgments are a compliance requirement, not a style choice. Use the chart. When a piece could be read as program-funded, include the full acknowledgment.

Center, program, and partner marks are pulled from official sources. We do not redraw, retypeset, or color-shift partner logos under any circumstance.
