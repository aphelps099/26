Glossary.

Stakeholder acronyms.

SBA

U.S. Small Business Administration

Federal funding agency of the SBDC.

SBDC

Small Business Development Center

A service center providing no-cost business advising.

OSBDC

Office of Small Business Development Centers

Federal oversight of SBDCs within the SBA.

ASBDC

America’s Small Business Development Center

A membership organization of 62 SBDC networks nationwide that provides collective professional development and a national voice in Washington, D.C.

Cal Poly Humboldt–SPF

Humboldt State University Sponsored Programs Foundation

Grant and research foundation that serves as fiscal host to the NorCal SBDC Network.

GO-Biz

Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development

State agency that funds the SBDC Program through CIP and TAEP grants.

Grant programs.

CIP

Capital Infusion Program

A GO-Biz grant that funds direct one-on-one consulting for clients seeking capital or laying groundwork for future capital plans.

TAP

Technical Assistance Program

A GO-Biz grant that extends the federal investment in California by expanding technical assistance funds — covers one-on-one advising, research, and a small percentage for marketing.

SBDC program terms.

EI

Economic Impact

The measurable effect of SBDC services on client businesses and the broader economy.

KPI

Key Performance Indicator

Another way to reference economic impacts within SBDC reporting.

Attribution

National accreditation standard

A national SBDC accreditation standard required to claim economic impact. Consists of written proof that there was a change to the client business and that the SBDC played a role in the change.

Third-Party Verification

CIP reporting requirement

A GO-Biz reporting requirement for CIP grant milestones of capital infusion or equity investment. Typically a copy of the first page of a loan or contract verifying the funder, business name, and dollar amount. Does not replace the need for Attribution.

Regional program acronyms.

TFG

Tech Futures Group

Specialty program serving high-growth and tech-enabled businesses across the network.

FC

Finance Center

Specialty program focused on capital readiness, lender relationships, and loan packaging.

RP

Restaurant Program

Specialty program supporting independent food and beverage operators.

CAHCC

California Hispanic Chambers of Commerce

Statewide partner organization extending the SBDC reach into Hispanic-owned business communities.

Finance terms.

FDIC

Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation

Independent federal agency that insures deposits at member banks.

FDC

Financial Disclosure Agreement

Document outlining a client’s financial position, used in capital readiness work.

FDI

Foreign Direct Investment

Investment made by a firm or individual in one country into business interests located in another.

CC

Cash Credit

Short-term financing arrangement allowing a business to draw funds up to a set limit.

IRA

Individual Retirement Account

Tax-advantaged personal retirement savings vehicle, sometimes referenced in business succession planning.

Loan terms.

EIDL

Economic Injury Disaster Loan

Federal loan program that provides working capital to small businesses suffering economic injury after a declared disaster.

PPP

Paycheck Protection Program

Pandemic-era forgivable loan program aimed at keeping employees on payroll.

PUA

Pandemic Unemployment Assistance

Federal program that extended unemployment benefits to self-employed workers and others not covered by traditional UI.

7(a)

SBA’s most common loan program

Includes financial help for small businesses with special requirements — covers working capital, debt refinancing, and equipment purchases. Best option when real estate is part of a business purchase.

504

SBA 504 loan program

Long-term, fixed-rate financing of up to $5 million for major fixed assets that promote business growth and job creation.

SBA Microloan

Loans up to $50,000

Program providing loans up to $50,000 to help small businesses and certain not-for-profit childcare centers start up and expand. Average microloan is about $13,000.

SBDC tools & programs.

IMS

Information Management System

Internal intranet for SBDC operations.

CMS

Content Management System

The platform used to publish and maintain web content across the network’s sites.

CRM

Client Relationship Management

A database with client records to understand and manage the lifecycle of a business relationship.

Neoserra

Network CRM platform

The NorCal SBDC network’s CRM platform for client records and session tracking.
