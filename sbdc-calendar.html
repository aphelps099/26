Three big moments.

Three weeks each year carry the loudest message and the most outside eyes. Coordinate four weeks ahead. Localize the audience, never the headline.

Q1

SBDC Day

Q1 · March 18

A nationwide celebration of the SBDC network. 16 centers, one drumbeat — the day we all post in unison and tag everyone who matters.

Audience

Small business owners considering advising. Partner institutions and program sponsors.

What we share

Client success stories pulled from every SBDC program — capital, technology, exporting, advising. America’s SBDC national impact stats card.

Who we tag

@AmericasSBDC · @CaliforniaSBDC · partner CDFIs · host institutions.

Call to action

“Find your center.” Link to the center finder — the highest-converting CTA we run all year.

Q2

Small Business Week

Q2 · Early May

SBA’s flagship week and our loudest legislative moment. Client stories travel up to district offices; impact sheets travel out to the press.

Audience

California legislators and their staff. Press. Small business community statewide.

What we share

Client success stories tagged to CA congressional districts. District-level impact sheets — capital deployed, jobs created, businesses advised, by district.

Who we tag

CA Senators and House delegation by district. @SBAgov. @SenSmallBiz. Local chambers and EDCs.

Call to action

“Tell your story. We’ll amplify it.” Link to the client testimonial form.

Q4

Annual Impact Report

Q4 · October

The year, told in numbers and stories. The partner audience reads this one closest — it sets the next year’s funding conversations.

Audience

Partner institutions, foundation funders, board members, state agencies. The people who fund the next year.

What we share

Network stories. Year impact — capital, jobs, businesses, advisors. Vision and priorities for the next twelve months.

Who we tag

Host universities · lending partners · foundation funders · state agencies · board.

Call to action

“Read the year. Help us write the next.” Link to the partner inquiry form.

Always-on rhythm.

Between the quarterly campaigns, five series keep our channels alive and on-brand. The heartbeats — small, reliable, unmistakably us.

Bi-weekly
Client Spotlight
A client success story with photo, quote, and one impact stat. Uses the Success Story template.
Weekly
Advisor Tip
A short, actionable piece of advice from an advisor. Under 200 words. Name and headshot included.
Monthly
By the Numbers
One compelling stat with context — $12M in capital accessed by NorCal businesses in March alone.
Rolling
Event Promotion
Starts two weeks before every event. Frequency steps up in the final three days. Date, time, registration.
Monthly
Partner Shoutout
A thank-you to lending partners, host institutions, and community organizations. Extends our reach.
WHY · CALENDAR RULES

One drumbeat, sixteen centers. When 16 centers post the same week, the network is louder than the sum of the parts. Localize the audience, not the message.

Two weeks before. Three days hot. Every event launches messaging two weeks ahead. Frequency steps up in the final three days. Below that floor you’re shouting into the void.

Quarters lock four weeks out. Once a quarter is locked, the campaign assets are final. Center comms can localize beneath the headline — audience cut, host venue, language, registration link. The anchor message stays fixed.

Heartbeats run between. Always-on series fill the weeks the calendar isn’t shouting. They build the relationships campaigns convert.

Numbers refresh quarterly. Capital deployed, businesses advised, jobs created — pulled fresh on the first business day of each quarter. Stale numbers are a credibility leak.
