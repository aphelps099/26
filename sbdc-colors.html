Accessibility · WCAG 2.1 · ADA notes
Ratios measured against WCAG 2.1, the legal floor for ADA digital compliance under Title II (state and local governments) and Title III (places of public accommodation). AAA = ≥ 7:1. AA = ≥ 4.5:1 for body text and ≥ 3:1 for large text (18pt+ / 14pt bold). Default to Cream + Navy or Navy + White when contrast is in doubt.

WHY · COLOR RULES

Berry: two per section, max. One italic-serif word in a headline and one inline emphasis. Berry on a button is a bug. Never adjacent to signal at large scale.

Signal: the action color. Live links, action eyebrows, the manifesto field. If something on the page is asking the reader to act, it’s signal.

Evergreen: affirmation only. “We say” labels, success states, accordion eyebrows. Never action. Never emphasis.

Cream: the default canvas. White is reserved for inverted blocks. Never put a white card on cream.

No new hex values. If a design needs a tone we don’t have, that’s a brief to revise the palette — not a one-off override.

Shades: anchors only. Step down for surfaces, dividers, and quiet UI states. Never shade berry. Never tint type.
