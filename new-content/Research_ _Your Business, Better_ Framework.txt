Validating the "Your Business, Better" Framework — Evidence One Pager
A skimmable, research-backed evidence sheet for Northern California SBDC. Each pillar below is supported by institutional studies and market data.
Why it Works (Core Principle: Simplicity + Outcomes)
• Harvard Business Review: Simple, outcome-focused brand promises outperform complex ones across industries.
• McKinsey & Co.: The world’s top 40 brands with consistent, clear messages yield almost 2× shareholder returns over 20 years.
• Lucidpress (2021): Brands with consistent messaging see up to 23% revenue growth [1].
• Edelman Trust Barometer (2021): 81% of consumers require trust before purchase [2].
• CEB/Gartner: Simple brand messaging reduces decision paralysis — a factor in 40–60% of stalled B2B purchases.
BETTER — The Core Promise
• “Better” taps into universal improvement motivation without suggesting inadequacy (Frontiers in Psychology, 2022).
• Stanford Social Innovation Review: Simple nonprofit messaging increases credibility, donor confidence, and resource acquisition.
People — The Differentiator
• NerdWallet (2023): 84% of Americans prefer human advisors over robo-advisors [3].
• SCORE (2022): Mentored businesses have a 70% 5‑year survival rate vs. 35% without mentoring [4].
• Mercuri International: 99% of B2B decision-makers cite trust as critical in choosing suppliers.
Funded — Proof of Value
• America’s SBDC (2023): Clients accessed $7.7B in capital last year [5].
• Federal Reserve (2024): Only 41% of small business loan applicants receive full requested funding [6].
• Kansas Dept. of Commerce: SBDC ROI up to 31:1 — some states report 88:1 returns.
• Research: Capitalized firms have 50% higher survival rates and are 3× more likely to expand.
Connected — The Client Experience
• KPMG (2022): 46% of entrepreneurs feel isolated; 72% face mental health challenges [7].
• MIT: Entrepreneurs with strong networks grow 3.5× faster than peers.
• Chicago Booth: Networks improve access to talent, funding, and opportunities.
• Community participation improves mental health by 40% and reduces burnout [8].
Competitive Position (Why SBDC Owns This Space)
• SCORE → “Free mentoring” (service). Local consultants → “Grow your business” (vague). DIY platforms → speed (self-serve).
• NorCal SBDC → “Your Business, Better” = Human expertise (People) + Capital outcomes (Funded) + Community (Connected). Backed by institutional research and measurable results.
